Project Overview

Build a premium, cross-platform AI-powered fitness application with an immersive modern UI/UX focused on workout planning, workout tracking, health analytics, social engagement, AI coaching, gamification, and long-term fitness progression.

The app should feel like a combination of:

* Apple Fitness
* Nike Training Club
* Strava
* Duolingo-style gamification
* AI-driven personalization apps

The experience should be:

* Smooth
* Interactive
* Motivating
* Visually stunning
* Highly personalized
* Addictive in a healthy way

Use polished animations, rounded cards, gradients, interactive gestures, swipe mechanics, floating UI components, and premium transitions throughout the app.
